Subject: Pre-launch partnership inquiry — Stayloop (Ontario rental platform, not yet live)

Hello,

I'm building Stayloop (www.stayloop.ai), an Ontario platform for landlords and tenants: landlords publish their rentals, screen applicants and manage the tenancy; tenants search and apply with an AI assistant. We are preparing for launch and are not yet officially live. We are not a brokerage and we hold no MLS data.

Before we launch, I'd like to understand whether a data relationship with Rentsync is possible, in either or both directions:

1. Outbound syndication. Landlords will publish on Stayloop first; we would like to push those listings to the Rentals.ca network (Rentals.ca, Rentfaster, TorontoRentals), to Zumper, and, if you distribute there, to Kijiji and Facebook Marketplace. Do you accept a scheduled data file or an API feed from a platform like ours, and what are the terms and fees?

2. Inbound listings. Would Rentsync license an active-rentals feed (Greater Toronto Area first) for display on Stayloop, with full attribution and a link back to the listing on your site? Our assistant uses AI to match a renter's stated criteria (area, budget, bedrooms, pets) to listings; it does not rewrite listing content. If AI matching is a concern, please let me know and we will scope accordingly.

I'm happy to share our launch plan, expected volumes and a sample of our listing data format. Who would be the right person to talk to about partnerships?

Thank you,
Jason Han
Stayloop Inc. · www.stayloop.ai
