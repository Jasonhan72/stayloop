**Subject:** IDX/VOW data feed inquiry — Stayloop Inc. (technology vendor) with sponsoring TRREB member [Agent name]

Dear PropTx Data Licensing team,

My name is Jason Han, founder of Stayloop Inc., a Toronto-based rental platform (www.stayloop.ai). I am writing together with [Agent full name], a registered TRREB member (RECO #[number], [Brokerage name]), who would be the sponsoring member for a data feed; Stayloop Inc. would act as the member's technology vendor / Authorized Vendor Partner.

**What Stayloop is**

Stayloop is a residential rental platform for Ontario. Prospective tenants browse rental listings, request showings and submit applications; landlords and their agents screen applicants, prepare the Ontario standard lease and manage the tenancy. Users register with an email address, and every account accepts our Terms of Use on sign-up. The site currently shows the member's own listings and listings the member imports; we would like to replace the imported listings with a licensed feed.

**What we are asking for**

1. A residential lease (rental) data feed for the GTA under the PropTx MLS Rules — we understand this is available as IDX and/or VOW. We would appreciate your guidance on which licence fits the use described below, the application steps for the sponsoring member and for Stayloop Inc. as the vendor, the applicable agreements (Data Licence / AVP agreement), fees, and the technical delivery (RESO Web API) and any sandbox access.
2. Confirmation of our reading of the Article 8 display requirements, which we have already implemented (details below).
3. Answers to three questions where we would rather ask than assume (below).

**What we have already implemented on the site, following Article 8**

- 8.26 — The listing brokerage is shown on every listing card and directly under the address on each listing page, in the same type size as the listing facts.
- 8.25 / 8.07 — Every listing page and the browse page carry the "deemed reliable but not guaranteed" statement, the data source, and the copyright and MLS®/REALTOR® trademark notice.
- 8.24 — Listing content is displayed as received. Where a description is machine-translated into Chinese for our Chinese-language interface, the translation is clearly labelled as a Stayloop addition, the original text prevails and is available on the same page with one click.
- 8.28 / 8.29 — Listings from different sources are labelled, grouped separately and can be searched separately.
- 8.27 — Search results are paginated at a maximum of 100 listings per query.
- 8.09 / 8.10 / 8.11 — Our Terms of Use (www.stayloop.ai/terms) now contain the listing-data clauses: personal, non-commercial use; bona fide interest in renting, buying or selling; no copying or redistribution; no use of AI systems, automation or scraping by users; ownership and copyright remain with the listing brokerages and the data providers; the user authorizes Stayloop and the data-providing associations to audit compliance; no fee and no agency relationship arise from accepting the terms. Acceptance is required at registration (checkbox), existing accounts are asked to accept the current version on their next sign-in, and each acceptance is recorded with a version and timestamp.
- 8.19 — Our Privacy Policy (www.stayloop.ai/privacy) states that consent records and listing-page activity may be shared with the data-providing associations or MLS® systems for auditing or legal purposes.
- 8.13 — Anonymous visitors have read-only access; signed-in users' listing views are logged, so we can provide an audit trail on request.
- 8.12 — Listing pages have a dedicated space for the sponsoring member's name, brokerage and contact details.

We are happy to provide screenshots or a demo account for review.

**Four questions**

1. **AI assistance to users.** Stayloop includes an AI assistant that helps a signed-in user search and compare rental listings within the site (for example: "show me 2-bedroom units under $2,800 near Union Station"), using only the data licensed for display and only for that user's own search. Rules 8.01 and 8.18 limit use of the data to operating the VOW. Is this kind of in-site assistance acceptable under the licence, and if so, are there conditions (for example, no use of the data to train models, no bulk analysis, display of the same attribution)? We will not train models on the data and will not use it for any purpose other than serving the user's search.
2. **Translation.** Our interface is bilingual (English and Chinese). For Chinese-language users we display a machine translation of the listing description, labelled as a translation, with the original one click away. Does a labelled translation count as an alteration under Rule 8.24, and would you prefer the original to be shown alongside the translation by default?
3. **Mixed sources on one page.** Landlords also publish their own rental listings directly on Stayloop. We currently label, group and filter them separately from MLS® listings (Rules 8.28 / 8.29). Is there anything further you require when both appear in one search result, such as separate result pages or specific wording for the label?

4. **Contact routing on a listing page.** Under Rule 8.12 each listing page will show the sponsoring member's name, brokerage and contact details, and the listing brokerage is always displayed. Stayloop also keeps a directory of RECO-registered agents who have verified their registration with us, and a consumer can choose one of them to help with a showing or an application (Stayloop charges no fee and takes no referral). On MLS® listing pages, may that "choose an agent" option remain alongside the sponsoring member's contact, or should the sponsoring member be the only contact offered?

Thank you for your time. Please let us know the best next step — we can complete the member and vendor applications as soon as you point us to them.
